WEBSITE POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the website of Bipin Kumar Maurya handles information provided by visitors through the website.

This website provides legal information, legal awareness resources, articles and information relating to legal consultation and assistance in various areas of law.

2. Information We May Receive

You may voluntarily provide certain information when using the enquiry or contact features of this website.

  • Your name
  • Your mobile or phone number
  • Information included in your legal enquiry or message
  • Email address when you contact us by email

You are not required to provide unnecessary personal information merely to browse the publicly available pages of this website.

3. Legal Enquiry Form

The website may provide an enquiry form through which a visitor can submit their name, mobile number and a brief description of their enquiry.

Information submitted through the enquiry feature is used to facilitate communication regarding the enquiry and to respond to the visitor where appropriate.

Important: Please do not submit highly sensitive, confidential or unnecessary personal documents or information through the website enquiry form. Share only the information reasonably necessary for initial communication.

4. WhatsApp Communication

The website may provide a feature that opens WhatsApp to send a legal enquiry to the contact number provided on the website.

When you choose to use this feature, your communication is handled through WhatsApp and is subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy practices.

5. Website Articles and Legal Resources

The website may contain legal articles, legal information, resources and references to laws or legal materials.

Such content is provided for general information and legal awareness purposes and should not be treated as a substitute for advice based on the facts and circumstances of a specific legal matter.

6. Website Data and Technical Services

Certain website features, including the management or display of legal articles, may use third-party technical services or databases.

Such services may process technical information necessary to operate the relevant website functionality. Their own terms and privacy policies may also apply.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website does not intentionally require visitors to create an account merely to browse its publicly available content.

Third-party services used by the website may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies and technical requirements.

8. How Information May Be Used

Information voluntarily provided by a visitor may be used for purposes such as:

  • Responding to an enquiry
  • Communicating regarding a requested service or consultation
  • Understanding the nature of an initial legal enquiry
  • Operating and maintaining website functionality
  • Improving the website and its information resources

9. Information Sharing

Personal information provided through the website is not intended to be publicly displayed on the website.

Information may be handled through third-party communication or technical service providers where necessary for the operation of a website feature, such as WhatsApp or website data services.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, legal process or a lawful authority.

10. Data Security

Reasonable measures are intended to be used to protect information submitted through the website. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. External Links

This website may contain links to external websites or platforms, including social media or communication services.

Once you leave this website, the privacy practices and terms of the external service will apply according to that service's own policies.

12. Children's Privacy

This website is intended to provide general legal information and is not specifically directed toward children. Visitors should avoid submitting unnecessary personal information.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in website functionality, services or applicable requirements.

The latest version will be made available on this page.
